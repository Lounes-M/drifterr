          Welcome to Drifterr
          
              
          Your model didn't change — your conversation did. Drifterr catches AI drift before you hit the wall, and re-anchors in one click.
          
            
          

            
          
              Step 1
              
          Pick your provider
          
              
          Where your chats are relayed. Use that provider's own API key — nothing is sent to a Drifterr server.
          
              
          
            
          

            
          
              Step 2
              
          Connect your tool
          
              
          Point your AI tool at the local proxy (use your provider's own API key), then just chat.
          
              
          
                OPENAI_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8787/v1Copy
              
          
              
          
                ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8787Copy
              
          
              
          Using Claude Code? Nothing to set up — Drifterr watches your sessions automatically (no keys, all local). Just declare your intent and keep coding.
          
            
          

            
          
              Step 3
              
          State your intent
          
              
          Drift is measured against what you set. Name your goal and any hard rules — Drifterr flags the moment a reply strays. You can edit this anytime.
